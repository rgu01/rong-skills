# AI This Week: Cheaper Frontier Models, Enterprise Agents, and a Record Copyright Bill

**Coverage:** 2026-07-18–2026-07-24 (Europe/Stockholm)

## Executive Brief

Between July 20 and 23, Google shipped a cheaper Gemini 3.6 Flash, OpenAI opened its enterprise agent platform Presence, and AMD used its Advancing AI event to line up hyperscaler compute commitments — evidence that the frontier race is now fought on cost, deployment, and compute supply as much as on raw capability.
7月20日至23日期间，谷歌推出了更便宜的 Gemini 3.6 Flash，OpenAI 开放了其企业级智能体平台 Presence，AMD 则借 Advancing AI 大会锁定了多家超大规模云厂商的算力承诺——这表明前沿竞赛如今在成本、部署和算力供给上的比拼，已不亚于对原始能力的较量。

On the legal front, a U.S. federal judge granted final approval on July 20 to Anthropic's $1.5 billion settlement with authors, the largest known U.S. copyright settlement, setting a costly precedent for training on pirated works.
在法律层面，美国一名联邦法官于7月20日对 Anthropic 与作家们达成的15亿美元和解协议给予最终批准，这是已知规模最大的美国版权和解案，为使用盗版作品进行训练树立了代价高昂的先例。

### Google ships Gemini 3.6 Flash with a cheaper "workhorse" tier

- [ ] Interesting

**Event date:** 2026-07-21

**What happened**

On July 21, 2026, Google released Gemini 3.6 Flash alongside 3.5 Flash-Lite and a security-focused 3.5 Flash Cyber, with 3.6 Flash and Flash-Lite available immediately across the Gemini API, Google AI Studio, Android Studio, and the Gemini app.
2026年7月21日，谷歌发布了 Gemini 3.6 Flash，同时推出 3.5 Flash-Lite 以及专注安全的 3.5 Flash Cyber，其中 3.6 Flash 与 Flash-Lite 即日起在 Gemini API、Google AI Studio、Android Studio 和 Gemini 应用中上线。

**Why it matters**

Google says 3.6 Flash cuts output-token usage by about 17% versus 3.5 Flash on the Artificial Analysis Index, pushing the "workhorse" tier toward lower cost, while 3.5 Flash Cyber will be restricted to governments and trusted partners in a limited pilot.
谷歌称，在 Artificial Analysis Index 上，3.6 Flash 的输出 token 用量较 3.5 Flash 减少约17%，推动这一"主力"档位走向更低成本；而 3.5 Flash Cyber 将在一项限量试点中仅向政府和受信任的合作伙伴开放。

**Sources:** [Google — Introducing Gemini 3.6 Flash](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-6-flash-3-5-flash-lite-3-5-flash-cyber/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/21/google-releases-three-new-gemini-models-but-no-3-5-pro/)

<a id="story-anthropic-copyright-settlement"></a>

### Judge grants final approval to Anthropic's record $1.5B author settlement

- [ ] Interesting

**Event date:** 2026-07-20

**What happened**

On July 20, 2026, Judge Araceli Martínez-Olguín of the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of California granted final approval to Anthropic's $1.5 billion class-action settlement, which pays about $3,000 per work across roughly 500,000 books pirated from libraries such as LibGen to train Claude.
2026年7月20日，美国加利福尼亚州北区联邦地区法院法官 Araceli Martínez-Olguín 对 Anthropic 的15亿美元集体诉讼和解协议给予最终批准；该协议就约50万本从 LibGen 等盗版库中获取、用于训练 Claude 的书籍，向每部作品支付约3000美元。

**Why it matters**

It is the largest known U.S. copyright settlement and signals that using pirated corpora for model training can carry nine-figure liability, raising the stakes for every developer trained on unlicensed data.
这是已知规模最大的美国版权和解案，表明使用盗版语料训练模型可能招致上亿美元级别的赔偿责任，从而抬高了所有使用未经授权数据进行训练的开发者所面临的风险。

**Sources:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/20/anthropics-landmark-1-5b-copyright-settlement-is-approved/) · [The Authors Guild](https://authorsguild.org/news/court-grants-final-approval-anthropic-copyright-settlement/)

### OpenAI opens Presence, its enterprise platform for production AI agents

- [ ] Interesting

**Event date:** 2026-07-22

**What happened**

On July 22, 2026, OpenAI introduced Presence, an enterprise platform for building, governing, and monitoring production AI agents, with guardrails to block risky actions, simulation-based reliability testing, and output-quality monitoring, released through a limited general-availability program.
2026年7月22日，OpenAI 推出 Presence，这是一个用于构建、治理和监控生产级 AI 智能体的企业平台，具备阻止高风险操作的护栏、基于仿真的可靠性测试以及输出质量监控功能，并通过有限的正式发布计划对外开放。

**Why it matters**

OpenAI says Presence already handles about 75% of ChatGPT's inbound support requests, signaling a shift from selling raw model access toward selling managed, deployable agent systems for enterprises.
OpenAI 表示，Presence 目前已处理约75%的 ChatGPT 客服咨询请求，标志着其从销售原始模型访问权限转向为企业销售可托管、可部署的智能体系统。

**Sources:** [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/07/22/openai-introduces-presence-help-enterprises-build-ai-agents/) · [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/orchestration/openai-unveils-presence-a-new-platform-that-lets-enterprises-launch-and-manage-realtime-voice-agents-and-chatbots)

### OpenAI unveils "Project Camellia," a 3.2 GW Georgia data-center campus

- [ ] Interesting

**Event date:** 2026-07-22

**What happened**

On Wednesday, July 22, 2026, OpenAI publicly unveiled "Project Camellia," a 3.2-gigawatt data-center campus in Effingham County, Georgia, to be built in phases between 2028 and 2032, with reported investment figures ranging from about $20 billion in local filings to $30 billion in national reporting.
2026年7月22日（星期三），OpenAI 公开发布了"Camellia 项目"——位于佐治亚州埃芬厄姆县的一座3.2吉瓦数据中心园区，将于2028年至2032年分阶段建设，投资规模在当地文件所述的约200亿美元至全国性媒体报道的300亿美元之间。

**Why it matters**

The campus underscores how frontier AI now depends on multi-gigawatt power build-outs, and its private planning drew local opposition over transparency, foreshadowing tighter community scrutiny of hyperscale sites.
该园区凸显出前沿 AI 如今如何依赖数吉瓦级别的电力建设，而其此前的私下规划因透明度问题引发当地反对，预示着社区对超大规模数据中心选址的审查将日趋严格。

**Sources:** [The Current](https://thecurrentga.org/2026/07/22/20-billion-openai-data-center-to-open-in-effingham-county/) · [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/pro/openai-is-spending-usd30-billion-on-a-new-mega-3-2-gw-data-center-in-georgia-and-its-trying-really-hard-to-quell-local-opposition)

### AMD lines up hyperscalers behind EPYC "Venice" and MI455X at Advancing AI 2026

- [ ] Interesting

**Event date:** 2026-07-23

**What happened**

On July 23, 2026, at its Advancing AI event, AMD launched its 6th-gen EPYC "Venice" CPUs, Instinct MI400-series GPUs including the MI455X, and Helios rack-scale systems, and said Anthropic, OpenAI, Meta, Cerebras, AT&T, and Cisco are collaborating with it, with Anthropic committing to deploy up to 2 gigawatts of MI455X GPUs.
2026年7月23日，AMD 在其 Advancing AI 大会上发布了第六代 EPYC "Venice" 处理器、包含 MI455X 在内的 Instinct MI400 系列 GPU 以及 Helios 机架级系统，并表示 Anthropic、OpenAI、Meta、Cerebras、AT&T 和 Cisco 正与其合作，其中 Anthropic 承诺部署最多2吉瓦的 MI455X GPU。

**Why it matters**

The lineup and hyperscaler commitments position AMD as a credible second source to Nvidia for AI compute, with the Anthropic deal alone signaling gigawatt-scale demand shifting toward AMD's Helios platform from early 2027.
这一产品阵容和超大规模云厂商的承诺，使 AMD 成为在 AI 算力领域可与英伟达抗衡的可靠第二供应源；仅 Anthropic 的这笔交易就表明，自2027年初起将有吉瓦级别的需求转向 AMD 的 Helios 平台。

**Sources:** [AMD — AAI 2026: AMD Delivers Full-Stack Compute for the Agentic AI Era](https://ir.amd.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1294/aai-2026-amd-delivers-full-stack-compute-for-the-agentic-ai-era) · [Moor Insights & Strategy](https://moorinsightsstrategy.com/field-notes/amd-comes-to-play-a-quick-take-on-advancing-ai-2026/)
