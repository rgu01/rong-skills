### GPT-5.6 Sol completes an eight-stage autonomous malware investigation benchmark

- [ ] Interesting

**Underlying event date:** 2026-07-22

**What happened**

On July 22, SentinelLABS published a long-horizon reverse-engineering benchmark in which all three GPT-5.6 Sol runs completed an eight-stage investigation, while GPT-5.5, GLM-5.2, and tested Opus 4.x models did not complete the full sequence.
7 月 22 日，SentinelLABS 发布了一项长周期逆向工程基准测试，其中三次 GPT-5.6 Sol 运行均完成了八阶段调查，而 GPT-5.5、GLM-5.2 和受测的 Opus 4.x 模型均未完成全部流程。

**Why it matters**

The result suggests that project-scale recovery—correcting an error and propagating that correction through dependent artifacts—may be a more useful measure of agent capability than isolated benchmark scores, although the researchers still require senior human supervision.
该结果表明，项目级恢复能力——纠正错误并将修正同步到所有依赖产物——可能比孤立的基准分数更能衡量智能体能力，不过研究人员仍要求资深人类专家进行监督。

**Sources:** [SentinelLABS](https://www.sentinelone.com/labs/frontier-models-tackle-autonomous-long-horizon-malware-analysis/)

### Progress Software to acquire Domo’s AI and data platform business

- [ ] Interesting

**Underlying event date:** 2026-07-22

**What happened**

On July 22, Progress Software entered an agreement to acquire substantially all assets and certain liabilities of Domo, including its AI and data products platform.
7 月 22 日，Progress Software 签署协议，将收购 Domo 的几乎全部资产及部分负债，其中包括其 AI 与数据产品平台。

**Why it matters**

The proposed deal would add Domo’s cloud-native, governed data platform and more than 2,400 business customers to Progress, strengthening the data layer enterprises use to ground AI agents.
拟议中的交易将把 Domo 的云原生受治理数据平台以及 2,400 多家企业客户纳入 Progress，从而强化企业为 AI 智能体提供可信依据的数据层。

**Sources:** [Progress Software](https://investors.progress.com/node/28901)

<a id="story-nvidia-cosmos-3-edge-siggraph"></a>

### NVIDIA releases Cosmos 3 Edge for local physical AI

- [X] Interesting

**Underlying event date:** 2026-07-20

**What happened**

On July 20, NVIDIA released Cosmos 3 Edge, a four-billion-parameter open world model designed for real-time, on-device physical AI, alongside broader SIGGRAPH releases for simulation, creative agents, and synthetic-video detection.
7 月 20 日，NVIDIA 发布了 Cosmos 3 Edge，这是一款拥有 40 亿参数、面向实时端侧物理 AI 的开放世界模型；同期 SIGGRAPH 发布还涵盖仿真、创意智能体和合成视频检测。

**Why it matters**

Running multimodal world models locally can reduce latency and data exposure for robotics, vehicles, and industrial vision while making post-training and deployment more accessible across edge GPUs.
在本地运行多模态世界模型，可以降低机器人、车辆和工业视觉应用的延迟与数据暴露风险，并让边缘 GPU 上的后训练和部署更易实现。

**Sources:** [NVIDIA Blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/siggraph-news-2026/)

### Microsoft expands Azure AI and HPC infrastructure with AMD

- [ ] Interesting

**Underlying event date:** 2026-07-20

**What happened**

On July 20, Microsoft announced three upcoming Azure offerings powered by AMD technology: HDv2 for AI data systems, HXv2 for chip design and technical computing, and ND MI455X v7 for production-scale AI inference.
7 月 20 日，微软宣布三项由 AMD 技术驱动、即将推出的 Azure 产品：面向 AI 数据系统的 HDv2、面向芯片设计和技术计算的 HXv2，以及面向生产级 AI 推理的 ND MI455X v7。

**Why it matters**

The launch expands hyperscale alternatives to NVIDIA-centered infrastructure and gives AI teams more specialized choices across data preparation, agent coordination, EDA workloads, and inference.
此次发布扩大了超大规模云中 NVIDIA 中心化基础设施之外的选择，并为 AI 团队在数据准备、智能体协调、EDA 工作负载和推理方面提供了更专业的方案。

**Sources:** [Microsoft Official Blog](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/07/20/microsoft-expands-azure-ai-and-hpc-infrastructure-with-amd/)

<a id="story-siemens-acquires-precision-innovations"></a>

### Siemens to acquire Precision Innovations for AI-powered chip design exploration

- [ ] Interesting

**Underlying event date:** 2026-07-20

**What happened**

On July 20, Siemens signed an agreement to acquire Precision Innovations and add its AI-powered, OpenROAD-based early chip-planning software to the Siemens EDA portfolio.
7 月 20 日，西门子签署协议收购 Precision Innovations，并计划将其基于 OpenROAD、由 AI 驱动的早期芯片规划软件纳入西门子 EDA 产品组合。

**Why it matters**

Bringing AI-guided exploration earlier into system-on-chip design could help engineering teams evaluate thousands of architecture options sooner and improve power, performance, area, and time to silicon.
将 AI 引导的探索提前引入系统级芯片设计，有望帮助工程团队更早评估数千种架构方案，并改善功耗、性能、面积和芯片落地周期。

**Sources:** [Siemens](https://news.siemens.com/en-us/siemens-to-acquire-precision-innovations/)
